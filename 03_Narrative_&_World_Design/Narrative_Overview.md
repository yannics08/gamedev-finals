# World Setting
> [!info] Guideline
> Summarize the environment, visual tone, and primary narrative conflict.

- **Setting:** Define environment, era, visual aesthetic, and mood. Where does the game take place? What does it look and feel like?
- **Central Conflict:** Define the core problem driving the narrative and player objectives. What is wrong with this world that the player needs to fix?
- **Themes:** Identify 1-3 underlying concepts or emotional motifs that inform environmental design, character choices, and story arcs (e.g., Technological overreach vs. nature, human isolation, the cost of ambition.)
# Characters
> [!info] Guideline
> Give every major character a clear motivation (what they want) and a gameplay role (what they do for the player)

- **[[Protagonist]] (Player):** Define character background, motivation, and mechanical function. Who are they? What is their main motivation?
- **[[Antagonist]] (Villain):** Define opposition motivation and presence in gameplay. Who is stopping the player and why?
- **[[Key NPC]] (Guide/Vendor):**  Define support function. Who helps the player?
